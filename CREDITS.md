Original source archive: https://cemc.uwaterloo.ca/resources/past-contests . Every problem contains links to its original official contest PDF and solution PDF. A complete index is in `dist/data/problem-sources.csv`; downloaded official PDF addresses and SHA-256 hashes are in `dist/data/source-pdfs.json`.

The collection comprises 376 Fermat problems (2000–2025), 140 Euclid problems (1998–2025), and 84 Canadian Senior Mathematics Contest problems (2011–2025, excluding 2020). We excerpted questions and solutions as images, cropped whitespace and printer marks, and added IDs, preparation-track assignments, approximate topic tags, and study difficulty labels. The mathematical wording was not rewritten. The 2005 Fermat question 25 was excluded because of an official correction.

No AoPS Wiki content was used. Summit is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CEMC, the University of Waterloo, or MAA.
